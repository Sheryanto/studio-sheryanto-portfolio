Approach

How these films are made

Every piece here begins with a still image, usually a flat product shot on white, the kind a brand already has on file. No shoot, no set, no studio time.

The rest is built: the environment around the product, the light that was never there, the reflections, the movement, the sound. A bottle photographed on a plain background gets a room, a mood, and a reason to be looked at.

Some of the work above started from a client's own photograph. Some started with nothing but an idea. The method is the same either way.

About

22 years in television and media.

I started behind the camera, moved into news production, and spent over a decade running live broadcasts and digital content for major networks across the Middle East. I know what holds attention in the first three seconds, because I have spent two decades measuring it in real time.

Today I use that experience to make films for product brands that look and feel like television commercials. I write, edit, color, animate, sound design, and finish every film myself. One person, one vision, from the first idea to the final cut.
